ที่มา · where this comes from

The lunar day follows the published Thai calendar for พ.ศ. ๒๕๖๙ — Thai PBS, ปฏิทินวันพระ ๒๕๖๙ — anchored day-by-day and re-derived by counting, exactly as the Coucal Clock does. The Northern reading follows documented usage: Lan Na numbers its lunar months two ahead of the central reckoning (จ.ศ. = ค.ศ. − 638, turning at Songkran; see Chula Sakarat), the waning day is แฮม, the sabbath is วันศีล, and the full-moon festival names (ยี่เป็ง, เดือนสิบเป็ง …) are derived from the Northern month number. The year-animal forms ไจ้ and เป้า are attested directly on digitised Lanna astrology manuscripts.
